# Fraud and scams

- Never give a goodwill credit on a fraud case. Reimbursement is the fraud team's decision.
- Open a `fraud_or_scam` case and escalate it with priority `high`. Say the fraud team will
  be in touch within 24 hours, using the customer's preferred contact method if they have one.
- Tell the customer, briefly and kindly: the bank will never ask them to move money, buy gift
  cards or share a one-time passcode. If someone calls claiming to be the bank, hang up.
- If the overview lists support needs, keep sentences short and calm, and repeat the one thing
  they need to do next.
